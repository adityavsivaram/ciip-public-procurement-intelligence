# Limitations

Study #001 is a public-data research study.

The public data did not provide a complete corporate-style evidence stack. In particular, the investigation could not independently establish:

- requisition chronology
- PO creation timestamps
- approval trail
- delegated authority applicability
- item/SKU/specification equivalence
- complete contract rate-card entitlement
- invoice-level discrepancy
- goods/service acceptance
- business rationale for the observed same-day PO cluster

Therefore the study does not establish commercial leakage.

## Language discipline

The terms “signal”, “investigation priority”, “potential exposure”, “validated impact” and “recovery” have distinct meanings.

A stronger state requires stronger evidence.
