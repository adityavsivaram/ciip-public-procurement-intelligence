# CIIP Methodology

## Core chain

**Signal → Identity → Comparability → Corroboration → Reconciliation → Evidence Gate → Conclusion**

### 1. Signal
A deterministic or analytical pattern that deserves attention.

### 2. Identity
Confirm that the records being compared represent the same commercial context.

Typical identity dimensions:
- supplier
- contract
- commodity/item
- department
- PO
- date
- cross-system identifiers

### 3. Comparability
Determine whether the observations are genuinely comparable.

Price comparisons require attention to:
- item/specification
- quantity basis
- contract terms
- time period
- legitimate escalation
- service mix

### 4. Corroboration
Seek an independent evidence source that can support or weaken the signal.

### 5. Reconciliation
Compare related populations such as PO, voucher and payment data while preserving identifier and scope semantics.

### 6. Evidence Gate
Determine what conclusion state the available evidence permits.

### 7. Conclusion
Stop at the strongest defensible state. Do not promote a signal simply because it is interesting.

## Governing principle

> A signal should trigger investigation. Evidence should determine the conclusion.
