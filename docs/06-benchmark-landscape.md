# Benchmark Landscape

CIIP does not claim novelty for procurement anomaly detection.

Relevant public work includes:

- Open Contracting Partnership — Red Flags in Public Procurement
- Open Contracting Partnership — Cardinal
- Explainable anomaly detection for procurement fraud identification
- Public-procurement knowledge-graph research
- Government procurement anomaly-detection projects

The methodological opportunity pursued here is narrower:

> **Evidence governance around procurement transaction signals.**

The goal is to make the path from anomaly to management conclusion explicit, testable and auditable.
